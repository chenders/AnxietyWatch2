How this picture maps to the machine

The central living field is a visual translation of the current read-only stream, not a medical score.

  • The expanding green breath-field is the machine-reported respiration rhythm.
  • The teal mask ring is delivered pressure; amber escaping motes represent reported leak.
  • The lower trails show stored respiration, leak, and pressure history from the latest gap-bounded observation window. Gaps stay empty; this is not limited to when the page opened.
  • AHI is intentionally not derived here. Separate read-only event notifications are captured and session state is shown, but numeric AHI still requires trustworthy coverage and the device summary/report path.
