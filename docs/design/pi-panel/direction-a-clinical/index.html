Bedside panel — Direction A, “Clinical”

The black rectangle above is the panel at its true size: 800 × 480, exactly what the Hosyond 7″ DSI shows. Everything here is review scaffolding — load with ?kiosk=1 and it disappears.

Hold to silence: press and hold the bar for a full 3 seconds. Releasing early resets to zero — no partial credit, and a tap does nothing at all. Silencing stops the audio, for two minutes. The visual alarm never silences.

Resting: at clear, untouched for 45 s, every region but the tier line goes dark — about 1.5 % of the field lit. Touching anywhere wakes it for 20 s. Escalation wakes it immediately and nothing dims while an alarm is active. Use the resting button to see it without waiting.
