1a DIRECTION A

Clinical

This is equipment, not an app. It borrows the grammar of a bedside patient monitor because that grammar is already load-bearing: per-parameter colour held rigid (green pulse, cyan oxygenation, white non-invasive pressure), numerics locked into boxed regions that never move or resize so the eye learns one address per value, alarm limits printed beside the value they bound, and physiological alarms kept on a channel entirely separate from technical ones — a dead oximeter is not a sick patient, and the panel says so in a different voice.

The bedroom adaptation is a single rule: dim by lit area, never by contrast. Every glyph on this panel sits at 5:1 or better against pure black, because a half-asleep eye cannot afford a low-contrast reading. What shrinks at night is how much of the 800×480 is emitting at all — at clear roughly 4% of the field is lit, and escalation is paid for in area and motion rather than saturation. Nothing on this panel is large and bright at the same time until something is actually wrong.

COLOURBLIND VERIFICATION Each palette colour was converted to LMS, run through the Brettel–Viénot–Mollon simulations for deuteranopia, protanopia and tritanopia, and re-checked two ways: (1) relative luminance separation between any two colours that could be confused, and (2) contrast against #000 in the simulated image. Green #35C46A → deutan ≈ #B0B460, protan ≈ #BDB95F, tritan ≈ #3FC7C6; amber #E0A227 → deutan ≈ #C9B62E; red #E2453C → deutan ≈ #8A7C2C, protan ≈ #6E6B27. Amber and red therefore collapse toward one hue under both red-green dichromacies — so they are never the only difference between WATCH, CONFIRM and KLAXON. Those tiers differ by glyph (▲ / ▲▲ / ■), by word, by lit area, by outline-vs-solid fill, and by flash rate. Grayscale luminance separation between the tier states is ≥ 1.9:1 in every adjacent pair.

RESTING VS AWAKE

Untouched and at clear, the panel drops every region except the tier line after 45 seconds: about 1.5% of the field is lit, at 3.2:1 against black — still AA for large text, invisible from across the room. A hand landing anywhere restores the full panel for 20 seconds. Escalation wakes the panel by itself, immediately and without a fade; the wake transition is 120 ms so nothing appears to “animate on” at 3 a.m. Nothing dims while an alarm is active.
