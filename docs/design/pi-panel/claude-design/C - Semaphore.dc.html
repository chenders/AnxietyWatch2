1c DIRECTION C

Semaphore

A grayscale instrument. There is no colour code in this direction at all — the tier is carried by a single abstract form on the left: one mass, two bars, three bars, a full field, or a dashed empty outline. Shape is read faster than a word and survives everything a word does not: a wrong pair of glasses, a phone-lit room, a photograph, a colourblind eye, a printer. The right side is nothing but type, set in one family at two weights, with no boxes, no rules, no chrome of any kind.

Its position on the brightness problem: make the display brightness a hardware dial rather than a design variable. Because nothing here depends on hue and the state ladder is also a luminance ladder — 3.17 : 1 at clear, 6.99 at watch, 13.94 at confirm — the panel keeps its full meaning at 25% backlight, where a colour-coded design would collapse. Escalation is the panel spending more of its own contrast budget, and the one saturated colour in the system, at klaxon, is a deliberate break in an otherwise colourless object.

WHY IT CANNOT READ AS FINE “Fine” is a single filled block. “Can’t assess” is the same footprint emptied out — a dashed outline with a 116px question mark inside it, at higher luminance than the calm state, alongside the words CAN’T ASSESS. Solid versus hollow is the single most robust distinction available at three feet in the dark: it survives blur, defocus, grayscale, and every dichromacy, and it is the one shape transition in the system that no physiological tier uses. Trends stop dead at the last real sample with a hollow cap and a dotted continuation; the sensor line that failed gets a dashed underline and its age in words.

RESTING VS AWAKE

At rest the panel keeps only the glyph and the tier word, both at the calm end of the luminance ladder: the shape is the status light, and it is legible without any text at all. Touch brings the type in at 120 ms and holds it 20 seconds past the last contact. A tier change replaces the glyph instantly — the form on the left literally changes shape, which is visible from across the room without reading anything. Nothing dims while an alarm is active.
