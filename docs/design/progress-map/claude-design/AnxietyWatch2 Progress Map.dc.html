AnxietyWatch2 — platform migration

A Raspberry Pi 5 becomes the system of record for the whole health platform: physiological capture, journaling, medications, prescriptions, pharmacies, lab results, CPAP data, and the overnight CNS-depression alarm. Generation one — iOS + watchOS plus a Flask/Postgres server on megadude — consolidates onto the Pi. The phone survives as a HealthKit bridge, a journal, and a viewer.
