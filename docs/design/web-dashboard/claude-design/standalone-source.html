DIRECTION A — CLINICAL

Built on the grammar of a hospital patient monitor — IntelliVue, CARESCAPE, Dräger. Per-parameter colour is fixed for the life of the product and never reused for status; numerics live in boxed regions at constant positions so the eye learns where to land; alarm limits are printed next to the value they bound; physiological and technical alarms occupy separate, priority-ranked channels; nothing appears without a timestamp. It is deliberately un-softened: the reward for looking like equipment is that it is legible from across a dark room and immediately readable by anyone who has worked a floor.

3 · INDETERMINATE — THE THIRD STATE

Not measured is not OK. The unknown treatment is periwinkle #a5b4fc — outside both the alarm reds/ambers and the reassurance greens — and it never appears alone. Four cues always fire together:

  1. Diagonal hatch fill across the whole region — texture, not hue.
  2. The glyph ? in the position where ✓ or ! would sit.
  3. The value replaced by — —, never a last-known number.
  4. An age counter that keeps climbing, plus a technical alarm that cannot be silenced, only acknowledged.

In charts the line stops. A gap is drawn as hatch between two dashed vertical walls; no segment ever bridges it. A continuous line across missing samples would be a claim the system cannot make.

7 · CHART RULES & ACCESSIBILITY LEDGER

  • No dual-axis charts anywhere — every measure gets its own lane with a fixed vertical scale and printed hi/mid/lo labels.
  • Gridlines at 1 px in --line (recessive by design, decorative only); meaningful boundaries use --line-strong at 3.5:1.
  • Direct labels only at lane extremes and marked events; never a number on every point.
  • Legend present on every plot where ≥ 2 series or ≥ 2 mark types appear.
  • Sequential encodings: one hue, light→dark. Diverging: signal hue ↔ periwinkle with a neutral mid. No rainbow ramps.
  • Status never rests on hue alone: glyph (✓ ! ? ×), fill-vs-outline, mono label and fixed position all carry it (WCAG 1.4.1).
  • Text ≥ 16 px body, ≥ 14 px labels, ≥ 4.5:1; large numerics ≥ 24 px at ≥ 3:1 but in practice all ≥ 7:1.
  • Full keyboard path; 3 px periwinkle focus ring at ≥ 3:1 against both themes; alarm flash disabled under prefers-reduced-motion and replaced with a static fill.
  • Dark theme is primary and independently validated; the light theme is a separate ramp (own hexes, own ratios above), not an inversion.
