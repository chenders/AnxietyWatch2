Direction B

The Night Report

The bet: the hard part of this product is not showing numbers, it is stating what happened and how sure we are. So the night is edited like a news page — a lede that says in one sentence whether monitoring worked, one large annotated chart as the evidence exhibit, and numbered chapters that walk the reader through the events in order, each carrying its own citation of raw values and timestamps. Serif for prose, tabular sans for numbers, hairline rules instead of boxes, and a strict rule that every claim names its evidence. Where evidence is missing the page says so in words as well as in hatch — a chapter titled "six minutes we cannot account for" is more honest than a smooth line.

The third state, in words and in texture

Measured-and-fine, measured-and-wrong, and not-measured are three different sentences. The third gets its own voice: periwinkle #aeb6e6, 45° hatch, the glyph ?, an em-dash pair instead of a number, and — unique to this direction — a written clause. The page will say "we cannot assess" out loud.

Space scale 4 · 8 · 12 · 18 · 30 · 34 · 72 px, on a 6 px rhythm. Prose measures cap at 74ch; numbers never fall below 14 px; all figures tabular so a ticking value never reflows its neighbours.

Rules this direction holds itself to

  • Never a dual-axis chart: separate lanes, own fixed scales, one shared x-axis.
  • Never interpolate. A gap is hatch between dashed walls, plus a sentence naming its duration.
  • Direct labels at lane extremes and annotated events only — no number on every point.
  • Legend on every plot with two or more marks; annotation numerals tie chart to prose.
  • Sequential ramps use one hue light→dark; diverging uses signal hue ↔ periwinkle across a neutral grey.
  • Body 16–17 px, labels 14 px, all pairs ≥ 4.5:1 (large display type ≥ 7:1 in practice).
  • Keyboard-complete; 3 px periwinkle focus ring; no motion beyond a 120 ms opacity fade, and none at all under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Dark is primary; the light theme re-derives every hue against #f5f7fa with its own ratios listed above.
