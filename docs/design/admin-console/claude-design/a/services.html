Restart aw2-spool-drain?

The drain stops for the duration of the restart. Nothing is lost — the spool is the buffer — but the backlog grows while it is down.

  • 14,208 rows currently queued. Expect roughly 700 more during a 2-minute restart.
  • The in-flight batch is rolled back, not partially committed.
