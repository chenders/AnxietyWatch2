Attempt history

Silent and failing are different diagnoses. This component exists to tell them apart.

Why this component exists

The product claims to replace impressions with evidence. That claim fails silently in exactly one way: a source stops producing and nothing says so, because nothing errored. A collector that never started reports no errors. A timer that stops firing raises nothing. Both leave a subsystem that looks like it had a quiet week.

So this component's first job is not to list runs. It is to answer one question before anything else: is this thing trying? Failing and silent are different diagnoses with different fixes, and the difference is the top line, in words, before any table.
