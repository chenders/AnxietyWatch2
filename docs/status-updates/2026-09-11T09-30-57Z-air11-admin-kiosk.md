## What was learned and changed because of it

- A loopback-only bind would not satisfy the required local-network workflow. The console now supports a LAN bind with a configurable private-network CIDR allowlist; peers outside the allowlist are rejected by the Rust API.
- LAN reachability makes a separate operator capability necessary for mutation endpoints. Read-only status remains available on the private LAN, while lifecycle actions require the externally provisioned console-admin token plus a per-process action token. The AirSense credential is not reused for console authorization.
- The existing repository has a physiology dashboard but no separate deployed kiosk implementation. The new kiosk therefore focuses on AirSense link/process/measurement/durability evidence and sanitized service logs, and is linked rather than merged into the physiology view.
- “Last status” alone was insufficient for troubleshooting. The page now shows the status blocker, counters, service state, recent error/warning/info journal entries, and audit availability, with important failures promoted above routine facts.
