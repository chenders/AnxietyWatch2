# H10 zero-heart-rate debugging

- Confirmed `vitals-h10.service` is active and the USB Bluetooth controller is powered and running.
- Confirmed the configured H10 is connected and exposes the Heart Rate GATT service.
- Captured a short transient BlueZ monitor trace in `/tmp` and removed it afterward. The H10 Heart Rate notifications were consistently two bytes `00 00`: Heart Rate Measurement flags `0x00`, followed by HR `0` bpm, with no RR field present.
- This matches the collector's decoder and explains the repeated `hr=0 outside registry range 20..250 bpm` messages. The collector is not fabricating zero values and is correctly rejecting them.
- No fresh H10 RR rows were present in the final two-minute check; older RR rows were from before the current zero stream.
- No service restart or Pi reboot was necessary; the fault is at the device/contact signal, not the BLE transport or decoder path.
- Reviewed the Polar Flow investigation specification and discovery notes. They document the same behavior: before first-use Polar Flow initialization, this H10 emitted `00 00`; after initialization it emitted nonzero HR and RR. The current collector follows the documented standard path (`0x180D` / `0x2A37`) and intentionally does not implement Polar's account/PFTP initialization path.
- The current `00 00` stream therefore matches the documented pre-initialization/device-state symptom, not a collector flag or byte-order mismatch. Reinitialize the H10 through the supported Polar Flow/Beat first-use path, then verify that raw notifications contain a nonzero HR and RR flag before rerunning acceptance.
